Each row in the dataset represents a customer and contains multiple attributes describing their spending and activity on platform.

A. Customer Demographics

  Feature                                                     Description

1.  ID--------------------------------------------------Unique customer identifier

2.  Year_Birth------------------------------------------Year of birth of the customer

3.  Education-------------------------------------------Highest education level achieved

4.  Marital_Status--------------------------------------Marital status of the customer

5.  Income----------------------------------------------Yearly household income

6.  Kidhome---------------------------------------------Number of small children in household

7.  Teenhome--------------------------------------------Number of teenagers in household

8.  Dt_Customer-----------------------------------------Date when customer enrolled with SmartCart



B. Purchase Behaviour (Amount Spent)

    Feature                                                    Description

1.  MntWines--------------------------------------------Amount spent on wine products

2.  MntFruits-------------------------------------------Amount spent on fruits

3.  MntMeatProducts-------------------------------------Amount spent on meat products

4.  MntFishProducts-------------------------------------Amount spent on fish products

5.  MntSweetProducts------------------------------------Amount spent on sweet products

6.  MntGoldProds----------------------------------------Amount spent on gold productsAmount spent on wine products



C. Purchase Behaviour (Frequency)

        Feature                                                 Description

1.  NumDealsPurchases----------------------------------Purchases made using discounts

2.  NumWebPurchases------------------------------------Purchases made through website

3.  NumCatalogPurchases--------------------------------Purchases made through catalog

4.  NumStorePurchases----------------------------------Purchases made in physical stores

5.  NumWebVisitsMonth----------------------------------Number of visits to website per month



D. Customer Feedback & Constants

    Feature                                                       Description

1.  Recency--------------------------------------------Number of days since last purchase
2.  Complain-------------------------------------------Customer complained in last 2 years (1 = Yes, 0 = No)
